Verified Revenue Report

4 Indian builders quietly making real money with zero funding, zero team

Every number below is pulled from TrustMRR, which reads revenue directly from each founder's Stripe or Paddle account via API, not a screenshot, not a self-reported claim. Here's exactly how each one got started, and what they're making today.

The builders

Web3Forms
$43,870 MRR · $357,916 all-time
A contact form API for static websites, built by Surjith S M, a front-end developer in Kerala, India. He started it in December 2020 because he was tired of broken PHP contact forms on client sites. It grew "really slowly" for four years, sitting around $800/month as late as January 2024. It's now bootstrapped, free-tier-first, and has 412,000+ users with 3,195 paying subscribers.
"This business is still a side project for me, I don't actively work on it." — Surjith S M
Hirevire
$13,766 MRR · $283,166 all-time
Async video-interview software that replaces first-round phone screens. Founded December 2021 out of Mumbai, bootstrapped, a 2-5 person team. Candidates record answers on their own time, recruiters review on theirs. Plans run $49-$249/month, 161 active subscriptions as of November 2026.
MagicSlides.app
$9,311-$9,829 MRR · $729,084 all-time
An AI tool that turns a topic, prompt, or document into a finished PowerPoint/Google Slides deck in seconds. Founded January 2023 by Sanskar Tiwari, who has shipped 24+ products and taught 100,000+ students to code over the past 5 years. Revenue peaked above $19K/month before settling into the current range; still verified via Stripe.
Supergrow
Ranked in TrustMRR's India top 5
A LinkedIn growth and content tool, one of the top-revenue startups on TrustMRR's India leaderboard (679 startups tracked, $5.8M total revenue on the platform for India alone). Same pattern as the others: one real, specific, boring-sounding problem, solved well, sold directly.
Source: trustmrr.com/championship and trustmrr.com/stats (verified Stripe/Paddle API data), cross-checked against each company's individual TrustMRR profile page and independent coverage (Untold Startups, 1M.chat) on 2026-11-13. TrustMRR tracks $1.98B+ in verified revenue across 70M+ transactions globally; 53.1% of founders on the platform have under 1,000 X/Twitter followers, meaning audience size is not the deciding factor in these numbers.

The takeaway: none of these four needed a funding round, a big team, or a large personal following. They needed one real, specific, personally-annoying problem, and the discipline to keep charging for the fix.
